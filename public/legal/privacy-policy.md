# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-29

This Privacy Policy explains what information Trade Data Converter (the "Service") processes and why. The Service is primarily free to use without creating an account, and performs conversions locally in your browser.

## Summary
- Files you convert are processed locally in your browser; we don’t upload or store your files on our servers.
- No analytics, advertising cookies, or third‑party trackers.
- Minimal operational logs to keep the Service reliable and secure.
- Optional sign‑in (when available) is handled by Firebase Authentication.

## Information We Process
- Files and content: processed locally in your browser. Not uploaded or stored by us.
- Operational data: basic event logs and error traces (non‑profiling) to detect abuse and improve reliability.
- Support data: content of messages or emails you send us.
- Account data (optional): if you choose to sign in, we process your email and UID via Firebase Authentication.

## Cookies & tracking
We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. We also do not use analytics or advertising trackers. Authentication, when used, does not set marketing or analytics cookies.

## Data storage & retention
- Converted files: not stored by us.
- Operational logs: short retention for security, abuse prevention, and debugging; then deleted or aggregated.
- Account data (optional): retained in Firebase until you delete your account (if accounts are enabled).
- Support communications: retained as needed to address your request.

## How we use information
- Operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
- Prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents.
- Respond to support requests.

## Legal bases (EEA/UK)
Where GDPR applies, our processing is based on: (i) performance of a contract (to provide the Service), (ii) legitimate interests (security, reliability), and (iii) your consent where required by law.

## Processors
We use the following service provider to operate the Service:
- Google Firebase: Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Hosting

We will update this section if we add or remove processors.

## Data transfers
Service infrastructure and processors may be located outside your region. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for cross‑border transfers.

## Your choices & rights
- You can use the Service without an account.
- You can delete your account (if enabled) and associated account data in Firebase.
- Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. Use the contact below to submit a request.

## Children
The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy. We will update the "Last updated" date when changes are made. Your continued use of the Service means you accept the changes.

## Contact
For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact: tradedataconverter@gmail.com
